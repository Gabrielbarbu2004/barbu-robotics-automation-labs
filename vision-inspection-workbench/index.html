Inspection rules

Nominal body: 90 × 64 px. Height: ±6 px. Equivalent hole radius: 7–13 px.

Reproducible batches

40 samples per batch: 20 good, 20 defective. Use calibration to tune, then evaluate on the separate seed.

UNDER THE HOOD

Pixels → regions → measurements → decision.

A seeded raster generator makes good parts and three defects: missing holes, offset holes and narrow bodies. Inspection uses an intensity threshold, 4-connected regions and geometric limits. Ground-truth labels are used only for scoring, never for the inspection decision.

Fixed camera view, isolated rectangular parts and simple noise make this a controlled experiment. Real inspection needs representative images, calibration, uncertainty analysis and independent validation. No machine-learning model or industrial integration is included.
