Recovery sequence

  1. Close the guard, release the emergency stop and clear any jam.
  2. Select Reset alarms to clear the latched stop.
  3. Select Start flow to resume from the saved part positions.

What this demonstrates

Deterministic sequencing, backpressure, part tracking, latched interlocks and deliberate restart.
